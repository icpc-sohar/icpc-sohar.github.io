Sohar University qualifies for ACPC

Team Error 418 has qualified for the Africa and Arab Collegiate Programming Championship, ACPC 2026 in Egypt, a first in the university's history.

Join nowRead the story

The club was founded in late 2025 under the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology. In its first year, its teams took the top three places in the North Al Batinah qualifiers, 5 teams with 15 contestants qualified for the Oman contest OCPC, and team Error 418 reached ACPC.

How the contest is won

A team of three students, one computer, and five hours to solve as many problems as possible. Every problem has a colour, and every accepted solution raises a balloon of that colour above your desk.
