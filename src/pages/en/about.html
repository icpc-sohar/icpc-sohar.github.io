About the club

The ICPC Sohar University Club is a student community for competitive programming, founded in late 2025 under the supervision of the Faculty of Computing and Information Technology. We train students to solve algorithmic problems, build teams to compete in the ICPC, and welcome anyone who wants to start, whatever their level.

Vision

To make Sohar University a recognised name in competitive programming, from Oman to the ICPC World Finals.

Mission

To help students learn programming fundamentals and problem-solving skills, and support them step by step until they qualify to compete at higher levels.

What we offer

Everything you need to go from your first line of code to your first contest.

  1. Training sessions

    Problem sets that grow from the basics to advanced algorithms, solved and explained together.

  2. Personal coaching

    A coach who follows your progress and suggests the right next step for your level.

  3. Contest teams

    We form three-person teams and prepare them for university, national and regional contests.

  4. Community

    Peers who share the same passion, internal contests and fun challenges.
