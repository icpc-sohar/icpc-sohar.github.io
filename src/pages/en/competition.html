What is the ICPC?

The International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) is the oldest and most prestigious programming contest for university students. Teams race to solve as many algorithmic problems as possible in the least time.

The official rule

A team of three students, one computer, and five hours to solve as many problems as possible. Teams are ranked by problems solved first; on a tie, the team with the lower time goes ahead, and every wrong attempt on a problem solved later adds a 20-minute penalty.

The balloons

Every problem has a colour. When your solution is accepted, a balloon of that colour arrives and is tied above your team's desk. Each balloon is a solved problem, and the more balloons you have, the higher you rank.
